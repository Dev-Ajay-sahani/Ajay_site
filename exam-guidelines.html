4. 🏛️ DigiLocker & State e-District Portals (Caste, Domicile, Income)

📁 DigiLocker (digilocker.gov.in): Manually uploaded documents limit is 10 MB per file (PDF, JPG, PNG). However, if you download a certificate from DigiLocker to submit to an SSC/College admission portal, always compress it under 300 KB – 500 KB as third-party portals cannot process large 10 MB files.

📜 State e-District Portals (Delhi, UP, MP, Bihar, Rajasthan, Maharashtra): Supporting documents (Aadhaar, old caste certificate, income proof, ration card) must be scanned at 200 DPI True Colour and kept under 50 KB – 200 KB per file (JPG or PDF).

6. ⚠️ Top 7 Reasons Application Forms Get Rejected (Avoid These!)
❌ 1. Signature in CAPITAL / BLOCK Letters
Almost every portal (SSC, IBPS, UPSC, RRB) immediately rejects signatures written in capital block letters. Always sign in your natural running/cursive hand.
❌ 2. File Size Just 1 KB Over the Limit
Government server scripts have ZERO tolerance. A 20.2 KB file on a 20 KB limit portal (like Parivahan or SSC) will fail upload. Always aim for the middle of the range (e.g. 15–18 KB).
❌ 3. Uploading Wrong Formats (PNG / HEIC / WEBP)
iPhone photos default to HEIC and screenshots default to PNG. Govt portals require strict JPEG/JPG. Use our Format Converter before uploading.
❌ 4. Wearing Spectacles in Photos
Spectacles (even with clear transparent glass) are now strictly banned across SSC, IBPS, and Passport Seva to prevent glare and biometric mismatches.
❌ 5. Using Outdated / Reused Photos
Portals mandate photos taken within the last 3–6 months. UPSC requires mandatory printed DOP, and SSC 2026 mandates live webcam capture during OTR.
❌ 6. Square vs Rectangular Photo Mismatch
Do not upload a 2×2 inch square photo where a 3.5×4.5 cm rectangular portrait is required. Aspect ratios are not interchangeable.
❌ 7. Inconsistent Signatures
Your uploaded scanned signature, the physical admit card signature, and the exam hall biometric signature sheet must match perfectly.
